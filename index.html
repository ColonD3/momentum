What kids learn

Three things in one class, because that is what the kids asked for.

Parkour

Getting over, under and across whatever the park has: tables, benches, walls, rails. It starts with how to land and how to fall, then jumps and vaults get built on top of that.

Kung fu

Stances, strikes and the focus that comes with them. Julius grew up in a kung fu school and teaches it the way he learned it.

Flips

The part they are most excited about. Front, side and back flips, worked up to in small pieces until the whole thing is ready.

How a class works

The practical stuff a parent needs before saying yes.

You can watch the whole thing

Class is outside at a public park. Stay on a bench, bring a coffee, see exactly what your kid is doing.

Skills come in steps

Landing and falling come before height. The ground basics come before any flip. Nobody gets pushed into something they are not ready for.

Ask us anything first

Bring every question you have before the first class. We would rather answer ten than have you guess.
